📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard – Power BI
📊 Project Overview

The Mobile Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance. It provides insights into total sales, transactions, quantity sold, average price, brands, customer ratings, and payment methods.

🎯 Objectives
Monitor overall mobile sales performance.
Analyze sales performance across different brands.
Track total quantity sold over the years.
Understand customer rating distribution.
Analyze transactions by payment method.
Provide interactive filtering for detailed analysis.
📌 Key KPIs
Total Sales: 68.28M
Total Transactions: 331
Average Price: 41.32K
Total Units Sold: 2K
📈 Dashboard Visualizations
Total Quantity by Year – Analyzes yearly trends in units sold.
Total Sales by Brand – Compares sales across mobile brands such as Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, and Xiaomi.
Customer Age by Rating Status – Shows the distribution of customers across Good, Average, and Bad rating categories.
Transactions by Payment Method – Displays transactions made through UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, and Cash.
